<section class="home-section" aria-labelledby="featured-project-title">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <span class="section-kicker">Featured technical project</span>
    <h2 id="featured-project-title">Seismic Correlation and Insurance Loss</h2>
    <p>A transparent earthquake catastrophe-risk workflow built to examine how dependence assumptions propagate from ground motion into insured and retained portfolio loss.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="featured-project">
    <div>
      <div class="tag-list">
        <span class="tag">USGS NSHM 2018</span>
        <span class="tag">Spatial correlation</span>
        <span class="tag">Insurance</span>
        <span class="tag">Reinsurance</span>
        <span class="tag">Parametric risk</span>
      </div>

      <p class="project-summary">The 13-notebook model connects rupture-level annual rates, a stochastic event catalog, ground-motion fields, structural and nonstructural damage, policy terms, occurrence excess-of-loss reinsurance, and parametric basis-risk analysis.</p>

      <div class="result-callout">
        Under the same synthetic occurrence reinsurance program, the modeled 2,500-year retained AEP PML increased from <strong>$19.36 million</strong> in the independent case to <strong>$33.27 million</strong> and <strong>$34.08 million</strong> under two spatial-correlation models. These are conditional demonstration results, not placement recommendations.
      </div>
