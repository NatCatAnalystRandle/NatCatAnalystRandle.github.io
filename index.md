    <h2 id="current-research">Understanding regional disaster consequences</h2>
    <p>Two buildings can experience the same earthquake without behaving independently. I examine how relationships across locations and model stages affect the consequences we estimate for a community.</p>

<section class="editorial-section" aria-labelledby="featured-project">
  <p class="section-label">Independent project</p>
  <div class="section-body project-feature">
    <div>
      <h2 id="featured-project">Seismic Correlation and Insurance Loss</h2>
      <p class="project-facts">470 buildings / 2 million simulated years / 13 notebooks</p>
      <p>An earthquake catastrophe-risk workflow connecting USGS seismic sources to building damage, insured loss, reinsurance requirements, and parametric basis risk.</p>
      <p>Using a common event catalog, I compare how two spatial-correlation models change portfolio tails and the protection provided by the same reinsurance terms.</p>
