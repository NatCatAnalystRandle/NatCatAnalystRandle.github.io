## Why the research matters

Regional risk estimates often aggregate consequences across many buildings, locations, and model stages. If the dependence among those elements is ignored or represented poorly, expected losses, tail consequences, recovery needs, and resource decisions may be distorted.

My work therefore treats correlation as part of the model architecture rather than as a final adjustment. The objective is not simply to make a model more complicated. It is to identify which dependencies materially affect decisions and which do not.

## Research and portfolio work

My doctoral research and my independent catastrophe-risk project are connected by a common interest in uncertainty and dependence, but they serve different purposes.

- **Doctoral research** focuses on methodological questions in earthquake and tsunami damage, consequences, and community resilience.
- **Independent portfolio work** extends related modeling ideas into insurance, reinsurance, portfolio loss, and parametric risk-transfer applications.
