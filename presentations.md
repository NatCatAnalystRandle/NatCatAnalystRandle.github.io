## Research Presentations

### Seismic Damage Assessment of Critical Facilities Accounting for Spatially Correlated Ground Motion Intensities

**13th National Conference on Earthquake Engineering (13NCEE)**  
Portland, Oregon · July 13–17, 2026  
**Oral Presentation · Conference Paper ID 4507**

**Emmanuel Randle and Andre R. Barbosa**

Presented a seismic damage-assessment framework examining how spatially correlated ground-motion intensities influence damage estimates for critical facilities.

### Impact of Spatial Correlation on Population Dislocation Estimates for Cascadia Subduction Zone Earthquake

**NHERI Computational Symposium**  
Berkeley, California · May 28–29, 2026  
**Poster Presentation**

**Emmanuel Randle and Andre R. Barbosa**

Examined how spatial dependence in earthquake ground motion affects estimates of post-earthquake population dislocation under Cascadia Subduction Zone earthquake scenarios.
